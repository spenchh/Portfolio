Current implementation

The FIFO tracks read and write pointers and occupancy in one clock domain. Writes are accepted when space is available; reads are accepted when data is available. Simultaneous accepted operations leave occupancy unchanged. The output register holds the last accepted read.

The initial cocotb test checks reset, one write, and one read with a data comparison. Full/empty boundaries, pointer wraparound, multi-word ordering, and randomized traffic are the next verification steps.

Planned test system

  • A configurable traffic generator for numbered records, bursts, and gaps.
  • A fault injector for dropped records and selected payload-bit corruption.
  • A programmable sink for receiver stalls and an independent scoreboard.
  • Statistics and host control for repeatable experiments.
  • An asynchronous FIFO and FPGA demonstration after the single-clock version is verified.

Connection to research

This project develops the buffering and verification skills behind my research exploration of stateful FPGA datapaths. Feedback-driven search is a candidate research extension; the current tester is at its initial functional milestone.
