Digital Design Intern at NASA

NASA Marshall Space Flight Center · August 2026 – Present

I am developing a Basys 3 FPGA server power-telemetry system for voltage and current measurements, combining analog sensing, a custom PCB, and VHDL.

The schematic, PCB layout, and routing are complete. The VHDL top level selects voltage or current display modes over UART using placeholder values while acquisition is developed. I am also developing UART responses and using cocotb with NVC for verification.

FAULTLINE: Speculative 100GbE FPGA Engine

VCU118 · Research in progress

I am researching how physical-link errors affect speculative packet processing, and how buffering and commit/cancel policies trade latency against data integrity.

The work is at an early stage, starting with error observability and capture feasibility. The hardware target is the VCU118, with single-lane validation before a 100GbE implementation.

SR-1 NEA VHDL

NASA · In progress

I am writing VHDL to arm and disable SR-1 NEAs, targeting the Microchip PolarFire FPGA Evaluation Kit (MPF300-EVAL-KIT).

The planned design includes an NEA control module and an ADC wrapper that passes current-sensing data to the controller. That feedback will support confirmation of NEA actuation. Development is ongoing.
